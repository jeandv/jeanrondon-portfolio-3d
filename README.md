## Loader, gate and audio

- `public/still.jpg` paints first (index.html preloads it) and is the LCP. The VCR OSD is laid onto its TV with a CSS `matrix3d` from the calibration. Without a still or calibration (first run, `?nostill`, data saver) the OSD runs in its "free" mode on a dark background. Once the room is live, the still and its `html.still` flag are removed.
- At 100% the OSD becomes the gate: CLICK TO ENTER (sound on) or ENTER MUTED. The click creates the AudioContext; the choice is kept in `localStorage`, and a second visit in the same tab session skips the gate.
- All sound is synthesised with Web Audio (`src/audio`): no audio files.

## Room details (phase 2A)

- **Desk lamp:** click it (no key binding). One shared `LAMP_MIX` (0 on, 1 off; `src/three/materials/lampMix.ts`) fades over 250 ms (cut with reduced motion) and drives the baked irradiance blend (`irrA` ↔ optional `irrB` lamp-off bake through `onBeforeCompile`; without `irrB` a group's `irrA` is scaled by 0.55), the greybox spot, the warm fill and the bulb (`lamp_bulb`, or a disc under `lamp_head`). The state lives in the store only; `?capture` keeps it on. A synthesized switch click plays.
- **Tape deck:** `#work/<id>` (notepad, keys or a click) sends that tape out of the rack, along an eased Bézier arc in front of the TV, flat into the VCR slot (the flap springs open, a thump); the TV shows INSERT TAPE until it clicks in, then a 150 ms static burst lands the tape channel. Another tape first ejects (soft click) and flies home; only one tape is ever off the shelf. Leaving #work keeps the tape in; #work without a tape ejects it. Pure state machine + path: `src/three/objects/tapes/{deck,path}.ts`.
- **Spine labels:** one 1024² canvas atlas (one row per slot) drawn from `src/content/work.ts` once the fonts are in: condensed Archivo title (auto-shrink, then ellipsis), VT323 years, label stock colour picked from the tape id, paper grain and worn edges. All labels are one batched mesh (one draw call); a Blender `tape_NN_label` plane replaces the auto decal.
- **TV light:** the TV canvas is sampled at 8×6 every 200 ms (and on channel changes), averaged in linear light and damped; it tints an additive glow card on the desk in front of the TV and, in greybox, a realtime spot. Blue INSERT TAPE throws a blue cast, a dark tape channel barely glows.
- **Adaptive quality:** drei `PerformanceMonitor` steps the DPR down the profile's range on sustained < 50 fps (the last tier also drops MSAA) and back up on recovery; after 3 direction flips it settles on the lower tier. Never in `?capture`.

## Room details (phase 2B)

- **BBS dial-up (#online):** the PC's contact menu sits behind a terminal program that dials JEAN-NET (`TERM 2.1`, the dialing directory, `ATDT 5550199`, two RINGs, the handshake, `CONNECT 28800/ARQ/V34`, an ASCII-boxed banner, an auto-typed `login: guest`), ~5 s, with the reference's status bar along the bottom (`TERM 2.1 | 28800 8N1 | ANSI-BBS | Dialing 555-0199 | Any key skips`, then `Online hh:mm:ss`). Any key (but Tab) or a click on the screen skips to the menu. The full run plays once per tab session (`sessionStorage` `jr3d:dialed`); later visits flash CONNECT; reduced motion and `?capture` go straight to the menu. The modem (real DTMF pairs, 440+480 Hz ringback, a 2100 Hz answer tone and a square-carrier + band-passed-noise screech) is synthesized on its own bus, low, and is cut the moment the dial-up is skipped or the PC leaves #online (`Channel.exit`). Script and timing: `src/screens/channels/pc/dialup.ts`; sound: `src/audio/modem.ts`.
- **Coffee mug:** `coffee_mug` (+ `coffee_liquid`, the coffee's top disc) on the desk left of the TV. A click lifts it toward the camera, tilts it at the lips for ~0.4 s (the slurp and the level drop land there) and damps it back (maath); five sips empty it (tooltip EMPTY, a ceramic clink), and it refills 75 s later or on reload. Steam: one camera-facing additive plane with two noise wisps, thinner as the coffee cools and empties; it rides on the rim and fades out while the mug is up (~0.1 s), back in once it is down (~0.6 s). Reduced motion: no lift, level + sound only. `src/three/objects/coffee/`.
- **Wall clock:** `wall_clock` with `clock_hour` / `clock_minute` / `clock_second`, on the back wall right of the PC monitor: the only bare wall a default view sees (home and #work see glass above the desk), so it reads in #about / #online. The visitor's local time; the second hand ticks once a second on a stiff spring (~10% overshoot); a very soft tick is heard only at the computer. A click takes the battery out (it stops on that time) or puts it back (a 0.9 s forward catch-up sweep, never backward). `?capture` shows 10:09:30. `src/three/objects/clock/`.
- **CV (was the credits card):** the CV lives at `RESUME.url` (`src/content/resume.ts`: `https://rxresu.me/jeandv/cv-jean-rondon`). That page sends `X-Frame-Options: DENY`, so it cannot be embedded: every entry point opens it in a new tab (`target="_blank" rel="noopener noreferrer"`, or `window.open(url, '_blank', 'noopener,noreferrer')` from a click / keydown). Four ways in: (1) the bottom-right **CV ↗** pill beside SOUND (`src/ui/ResumeLink.tsx`, a real `<a>` with `aria-label="Open Jean Rondón's CV (opens in a new tab)"`, the ↗ is an inline SVG); (2) a click on the `rolodex` (card file in front of the lamp; tooltip VIEW CV) floats an index card (canvas, Courier Prime, red head rule, blue rules) up to the middle of the frame (camera-relative `CardPlacer`, like the #work notepad) with the name and role from the profile, CURRICULUM VITAE, a one-line hint, a big boxed **OPEN CV ↗** button (a hit rect: tooltip OPEN CV, red under the pointer, the card stays up) and, as small print, "Credits:" with a short colophon of the full credits list (inspiration, music, fonts, icons, landmark). Enter while the card is up opens the CV too; Esc or a click anywhere else on the card files it back (tooltip PUT BACK CV). On screens ≤ 420 px wide the card is held upright in larger type (the rolodex is out of frame on a phone; the pill is the way in). Drawing and hit rects: `src/screens/cvCard.ts` + `cvCardLayout.ts` (import-free, so e2e can aim at the button), rig `src/three/objects/cv/rig.ts`; (3) the BBS menu on #online ends with `[4] CV  rxresu.me/jeandv/cv-jean-rondon` (built in `src/content/contactMenu.ts` from `RESUME`, so `links.ts` stays untouched); (4) the HTML layer: a CV pill under the intro, a row in Online and a footer line (all three visible in the `?lite` / no-WebGL site, where the 3D pills are hidden). The full credits list stays in the HTML layer's Credits section (`src/content/credits.ts` + `creditsExtra.ts`).
- **Raindrops on the glass:** a second mesh on the `window_glass` geometry (premultiplied over the multiplicative tint, one draw call): two hash grids of static beads (darker rim, the street glow upside down in the upper half, a lamp highlight; the largest 8% may just reach the bloom threshold) and, now and then, a drop running down its lane stick-slip with a short beaded trail. Reduced motion: beads only. `src/three/materials/glass.ts`.
- **Reverb:** a ConvolverNode with a generated stereo impulse response (`(rand·2−1)·(1−i/len)^3.2·0.6`, 0.9 s) returning into the master. Each one-shot (tick, click, tape, lamp, sip, clink, clock, card, modem…) plays into a bus with a 0.12–0.18 wet send; the rain stays dry. Built inside the unlocking gesture, like the rest of the graph. `src/audio/reverb.ts`.

## Room details (phase 2C)

- **CD player + music (key M):** `cd_player` (a discman in front of the tape rack, with the TV, VCR and tapes: the media corner) with `cd_lid` (hinged at the back), `cd_disc` and `cd_display`. M, or a click on it, flies to a close-up (a camera mode in the store like the TV zoom, so the URL keeps its page; `body[data-camera-mode="cd"]`); Esc, M or a click away returns. Up close: the lid opens / shuts on a click (spring; opening stops the disc, like a real one), the LCD strip is a canvas screen (seven-segment track and time, play / pause icon, REP, MUTE, the title scrolling a character at a time in VT323) with PREVIOUS, PLAY/PAUSE and NEXT as hit rects; Space / Enter play-pause, ←→ change track, 1–3 pick one. The disc spins up and down with play (it shows through the lid's window). Leaving the close-up shuts the lid; the music keeps playing. Reduced motion: no lid or disc motion, just the state. `?capture`: closed and stopped. `src/three/objects/{CdPlayer.tsx,cd/}`, `src/screens/channels/cd/`.
- **Music (0 KB of files):** `src/audio/music/`. Three seeded tracks (`tracks.ts`; titles in `src/content/music.ts`, placeholders): 70–85 BPM, an intro / A / B / A / B / outro form of 40 bars. `compose.ts` writes each one deterministically from its seed: jazzy ii–V–I-flavoured progressions (rootless 9th/13th voicings, voice-led around E4) on a two-operator FM electric piano with a slow tape wow, a triangle bass with chromatic approach notes, a lazy kit (sine kick, band-passed noise snare with ghost notes, noise hats, swung 16ths, fills at phrase ends) and a sparse pentatonic melody (call and answer) in the B sections; a very low disc hiss. `scheduler.ts` is the classic look-ahead scheduler (a 25 ms `setInterval` booking every note due within 120 ms on the AudioContext clock; contiguous half-open windows, so nothing is booked twice; stopped while paused). `transport.ts` maps the CD player's state (`src/three/objects/cd/player.ts`: lid, play / pause, track, elapsed; repeat-all) onto the audio clock, so the LCD, the store and the notes agree and a hidden tab (suspended context) freezes the disc with the sound. The music goes through the master and its compressor with a 0.14 reverb send; SOUND OFF silences it (no notes are booked while muted, the disc keeps its place); the rain ducks ~35% while it plays. It never starts on its own.
- **Desk calculator:** `calculator` (+ `calc_display`, `calc_keys`) between the rolodex and the mug. A click lifts it to a camera-relative pose facing the camera (the notepad / credits `CardPlacer`; tooltip USE / PUT DOWN CALCULATOR) and it works: its keys are canvas hit rects (a pressed key shows a darker cap for 100 ms with a soft click) or the physical keys while it is up (digits, `+ - * / . , = Enter Backspace`, Delete = CE, c = C); Esc clears it, a second Esc (or a click anywhere else) puts it down. Ten-digit grey-green LCD in seven segments; `58008` and `0.7734` get a tiny wink. The logic is a pure four-function, immediate-execution engine (`src/three/objects/calc/engine.ts`: chaining, `=` repeat, ±, CE / C, backspace, divide by zero and overflow → `E`).
- **PC keyboard:** at the computer (#about / #online, nothing held), each physical key press sinks its cap ~2 mm and springs it back on release: 104 procedural caps (a US layout fitted to `pc_keyboard`'s top face) in one `InstancedMesh` (one draw call), addressed by `KeyboardEvent.code`. Blender `pc_key_<code>` nodes are pressed instead when present. The #about / #online framing now looks a little lower (and wider) so the keyboard shows along the bottom of the frame.
- **City traffic:** an S-Bahn crosses the far skyline on an elevated line every 30–90 s (lit windows, a headlight with its beam, a red tail light, partly hidden by the near blocks), and about one plane a minute (never more than two) crosses the sky with red / green nav lights, a double white strobe and a red beacon, dimmed inside the clouds. Both are pure functions of the shared window clock (`src/three/materials/cityTraffic.ts`, the same constants in TS and GLSL) drawn inside the existing far-skyline and sky shaders: no extra draw calls, no per-frame JS. Reduced motion runs the window clock at 0.4×, so they come slower and rarer.

## Blender → web asset pipeline (M3)

Step by step in [`docs/BLENDER.md`](docs/BLENDER.md). In short:

1. `blender/scripts/prep_bake.py` prepares one bake group at a time (custom property `bake`, UV maps `[UVMap, BakeUV]`, a `BAKE_TARGET` image node, Cycles settings) and can bake + save `blender/bake/<group>_<albedo|irrA>.png`.
2. `blender/scripts/export_prep.py`, on a `*_export.blend` copy, applies modifiers, joins the statics into `static_<group>`, sets one `M_<group>` material per baked object and validates the naming contract.
3. Export glTF to `blender/export/room.raw.glb`, then `npm run assets`.

`npm run assets:glb` runs `gltf-transform optimize` with the plan's flags plus `--prune false` (the plan's flags alone prune the `anchor_*` Empties), records the authored origin of `vcr_flap`, of the clock hands and of `cd_lid` as the `hinge` extra (meshopt quantization moves node origins; the flap spring and the hands rotate around it), and refuses to replace the GLB if a baked mesh lacks `TEXCOORD_1`, a baked mesh is instanced, a baked node has children, or the contract fails.

The app switches a bake group from greybox to baked materials as soon as both `src/assets/bake/<group>_albedo.webp` and `<group>_irrA.webp` exist. An optional `<group>_irrB.webp` (the same bake with the lamp off; `prep_bake.py --pass irrB`) is fetched after the room is live, outside the boot byte count (`sizes.json` lists it under `deferred`).

## Performance (dev)

In dev, `window.__renderInfo()` (browser console or Playwright) returns the last composed frame's draw calls/triangles, one scene-only render, the post-processing share, `renderer.info` programs/textures/geometries, the EffectComposer passes, a material census (greybox vs baked, lightMap channel and intensity) and the adaptive `quality` tier (`{ tier, tiers, dpr, msaa, settled }`). `window.__screenPoint('<node>')` returns where a GLB node is drawn (CSS px), for scripted clicks; `window.__screenPointUv('<node>', u, v)` where the point at UV0 (u, v) of its mesh is (e.g. a button on a canvas screen); `window.__screenPointWorld(x, y, z)` where a world point is (runtime objects that are not GLB nodes: the posters, the LED strip). ## Deploy (Cloudflare Pages)

- Connect the repo in Cloudflare Pages: **Build command** `npm run build`, **Build output directory** `dist`. Node 24 comes from `.nvmrc` (the Pages build image reads it; alternatively set the `NODE_VERSION=24` environment variable). Every branch gets a preview URL.
- `public/_headers`: `/assets/*` immutable for a year (hashed by Vite), `*.glb` as `model/gltf-binary`, `/` and `/index.html` `no-cache`, `still.jpg` / `og.jpg` cached for a day (stale-while-revalidate a week), plus `nosniff` and a referrer policy. Check with `curl -I https://<site>/`.
- `public/robots.txt` allows everything; the build appends `Sitemap: <SITE.url>/sitemap.xml` and writes `dist/sitemap.xml` from `src/content/site.ts`. While `SITE.url` is the `https://example.com` placeholder, the build prints a warning: set the real URL before launching.
- `public/favicon.svg` is linked from `index.html`.

## Known

- `THREE.Clock: This module has been deprecated` (console warning, three r183+): React Three Fiber 9 hard-codes `new THREE.Clock()` in its store and its frame loop drives `state.clock` (`getDelta()`, `elapsedTime`, `oldTime`; `VisibilityPause` relies on those too). R3F 9 has no option to pass a `THREE.Timer`, so the warning stays until an R3F release switches clocks. It is harmless.
